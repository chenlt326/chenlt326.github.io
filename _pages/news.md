**Jun 2026**

* I led an eScience Institute Postdoctoral Lunch Seminar at the University of Washington, titled “Google Earth Engine for Reproducible Earth Data Science: From Hourly Climate Grids to Population Exposure.”

**May 2026**

* Our paper, “Heat Metrics and Thresholds Reshape Population Exposure and Inequality Signals,” was accepted by *Environmental Research Letters* and featured as a <a href="https://csde.washington.edu/news-events/chen-casey-and-co-authors-show-that-heat-metric-and-threshold-choice-reshape-population-exposure-and-inequality-estimates/" style="text-decoration: none;">CSDE Research Highlight</a>. 

**Apr 2026**

* Our paper, “Comparing Designs for Photovoltaic-Green Roofs: A Year-Long Field Study in a Subtropical Climate,” was accepted by *Renewable Energy*.

**Dec 2025**

* Our paper, “Mitigating Urban Climate-Energy Feedback with Citywide Building-Integrated Photovoltaics Implementation,” was accepted by *Nexus*.

**Sep 2025**

* I joined the University of Washington as a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Urban Design and Planning.
* I was selected as a <a href="https://escience.washington.edu/member/liutao-chen/" style="text-decoration: none;">UW eScience Data Science Postdoctoral Fellow</a>.

* Our rooftop PV study was featured by <a href="https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/06/10/rooftop-pv-may-unintentionally-exacerbate-urban-heat/" style="text-decoration: none;">pv magazine</a>, highlighting the urban microclimate and heat-resilience implications of large-scale rooftop photovoltaic deployment.

**May 2025**

* Our paper, “The Resilience Paradox of Rooftop PV: Building Cooling Penalties and Heat Risks,” was accepted by *Building and Environment*.
